Cognitive Science

Mastering Complex Domains: A Daily Learning Framework For Physics and Programming

Mastering cognitively demanding fields, such as physics and programming, requires more than rote repetition; it demands a structured approach that aligns with human cognitive architecture. This report synthesizes findings from cognitive science and pedagogical research to present a daily three-phase workflow: Pre-study, Active study, and post-study. By integrating cognitive load theory (CLT) and Variation Theory, the framework optimizes the management of intrinsic and extraneous loads while fostering the development of qualitative mental models and robust logical structures.

Key highlights include the use of faded worked examples to transition from novice to expert performance, the "Bear Hunter System" (BHS) for conceptual chunking, and generative self-explanation to deepen the understanding.

Quantitative Finance Machine Learning Statistical Modeling

Evaluation of Statistical Arbitrage Models For Financial Markets

Several studies in the 90s and early 2000s have shown a decline in the profitability of pairs trading. It is not known whether this decline will continue further in the future or come and go. It has, therefore, become imperative to periodically evaluate the efficacy of the pairs trading approaches. Pairs trading generally has a number of approaches that include, but not limited to, (i) codistanced approach, (ii) cointegrated approach, (iii) time-series approach, (iv) stochastic control approach, (v) machine learning approach, (vi) copula approach, and (vii) principal component analysis (PCA) approach.

Key highlights include the use of machine learning integration in pairs trading for adaptive strategy optimisation.
